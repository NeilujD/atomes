# UI direction: the frame from Linear, the look from Shelf Check

Status: proposed, 2026-11-04; amended 2026-11-14 (colour roles, back arrow), 2026-11-15 (Shelf Check result screen, CORE-120), 2026-11-17 (one look, ADR 0018, CORE-121) and 2026-11-18 (Statistics, ADR 0019, CORE-143). Applies to every screen. Code: `src/styles/theme.css`, `src/layouts/AppShell.astro`.

Sources: Linear, ["How we redesigned the Linear UI (part II)"](https://linear.app/now/how-we-redesigned-the-linear-ui), 28 March 2024, for the frame; the owner's design "Atomes – Scan result" (CORE-120) for the look of every surface (ADR 0018). The screen designs stay the reference for content (`docs/design/source/`, canvas link in `docs/HANDOFF.md`).

## In short

Atomes keeps the canvas colours, font and screens. The frame comes from Linear; the look of every surface comes from the Shelf Check result screen:

1. **The chrome is an inverted L.** On desktop, the sidebar and the view header frame one content panel. On the phone, the same parts become a large title and a tab bar. The frame stays as written below (Q13).
2. **One look, in both themes (ADR 0018).** Every screen takes the Shelf Check look: soft glows behind the top of a screen, text and fills as levels of ink, filled buttons with 16 px corners, chips and cards. Every screen follows the Appearance setting, light or dark.
3. **Colour answers a question.** Cobalt fills the one main action of a screen and marks something new; green and orange answer ownership; a muted red marks actions that destroy. The chrome stays grey.
4. **Few inputs, the rest derived.** One `light-dark()` pair per canvas colour, one contrast value that makes a high-contrast mode, and a fixed set of tokens: a screen types no raw px, hex or alpha.
5. **Define behaviour before screens, then test it per view type.** The rules below come first; each new screen must fit them.

## The one look

**Main action (Q7).** The one main action of a screen is filled cobalt (`--accent-fill`), in light text (`--on-accent`), `--radius-button` corners, `--button-height`. `--accent` is a pale cobalt in dark, so the fill has its own pair (5.1:1 under white in dark, `theme.test.ts`). A screen shows at most one at a time; every screen with a main action shows it filled (Settings has none at rest: only the submit of an open inline form; adding an address, the address step steps back once the code is sent, as on Sign in). The Shelf Check result screen is the exception: its main action is filled with the answer colour (`--button-height-large`).

The frame keeps its own icon buttons (`.frame-icon` in `AppShell`, Q13).

**Ownership colours (Q8).** Green (`--not-owned`) says the reader does not own the book: not in the Library, a Missing Volume, read but not owned. Orange (`--owned`) says they do. They show only where ownership is answered: the Shelf Check answer, the Book page glow (green while the reader owns no Copy, orange once they own one), Series page spines, counts and glow, and To Buy (its glow, green: nothing there is owned). The "Not owned" mark is one shared chip, `.chip-not-owned`, in To Buy and the Library (Grid, List, Cover flow); in the Library the cover of a book read and not owned also turns grey. Text in these colours uses `--not-owned-text` or `--owned-text`; a filled button keeps dark text (`--on-ownership`) in both themes. A pill or chip tinted with the colour (`--alpha-tone-fill`: the Shelf Check answer pill, the "Not owned" chip) paints its tint on `--surface`, so neither a card nor a glow behind it lowers its contrast: its text keeps 4.5:1 (`theme.test.ts`). One exception: the orange ground of the Staging, Preview and Local app icons, which live outside the app and must look different from production.

**Glows (Q9).** Soft blobs drift behind the top of a screen (title and first section, or title and toolbar), never behind lists, covers or the Cover flow strip: cobalt on Home and the Library, green or orange where the screen answers ownership: Shelf Check, the Book page, the Series page (orange only when the reader owns every Volume the Catalog lists), To Buy (green). A big cover (120 px wide or more) gets `--radius-cover-large`, a halo and rings. The blobs, halo, rings and their motion are one shared part, `src/styles/glow.css`: a screen sets only its colours (`--tone`, `--tone-deep`), the box of its glow and where each blob sits; none of it takes a tap. Soft edges come from radial gradients on a padded box, never a blur filter. In light a glow shows at `--glow-light` of its colour. With reduced motion every glow stays still and rings do not show.

**Spines (Q10).** One vertical spine per Volume, `--radius-spine` corners, no outline, filled shades (`src/styles/series.css`): owned, not read, tinted orange (`--spine-owned`, the owned text colour at `--alpha-shade-medium`); Missing tinted green (`--spine-missing`, the not-owned text colour at `--alpha-shade-faint`); any other Volume not owned `--shade-faint`; read and owned full ink; read but not owned keeps its read mark (an ink dot) on `--shade-faint`. The current or picked Volume shows in colour with a glow. The current Volume is the Volume of the Book page the reader came from (`seriesHref(isbn, 'book')`); from anywhere else (Series Onboarding, the Library Series card, To Buy) no Volume is current. The segments of the Shelf Check Series card share these tokens (`theme.css`): owned `--spine-owned` (the bar of a longer Series too), read ink, read but not owned the dot; a segment does not know Missing, so a Volume not owned stays `--shade-faint`. The app icon and mark take the faint shade.

**The mark and the app icon (Q10, Q11).** One shape: two ink spines, the empty slot as a faint spine (`--shade-faint`, the "not owned" spine of the Series page, no outline, no dashes) and a cobalt spine. The animated mark (Flow screens, the gate) lifts the books and pulses the faint spine gently while it works (`--motion-pulse`), and the arrival book lands on the faint spine when done; failed stays still, the slot empty; reduced motion stops both. The app icon is flat: no blobs, no glow (at 32 px a glow turns to mud), no green or orange, on the app grounds in light and dark. Staging, Preview and Local keep the orange ground (Q12, the one exception of Q8). `bun scripts/icons.ts` writes every SVG icon under `public/` from `theme.css` and renders every PNG from its SVG; an SVG cannot read a CSS variable, so the script mixes the faint spine's hex from `--ink`, the ground and `--alpha-shade-faint` (`scripts/icons.test.ts` fails while `public/` differs). The Sign-in Code mail draws the same mark in HTML; the script also writes its faint spine for the light and dark grounds to `src/lib/server/mail-mark.ts`. Code: `src/components/Mark.astro`, `scripts/icons.ts`, `src/lib/server/mail-html.ts`.

**Small heading, icons, titles (Q11).** Section titles: `--type-small`, 500, capitals, `--tracking-caps`, `--text-heading`. Icons: stroke `--icon-stroke`, `--icon-size` in round buttons, `--icon-size-inline` inline. Titles take `--tracking-tight`.

**Desktop step (Q12).** With a pointer from 960 px wide, buttons step down to 40 to 44 px and round icon buttons to 36 px. Lists and toolbar rows stay dense: `--row` does not change.

**The frame (Q13).** Sidebar, view header, panel and tab bar stay as written below.

**Destructive actions.** A destructive action offers Undo in its toast, not a confirm dialog. An action that cannot be undone (Delete my account, Sign out) keeps the confirm dialog.

## Where Atomes differs from Linear, and why

| Linear | Atomes | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dense everywhere; keyboard first. | Dense with a pointer (32 px rows), roomy on touch (44 px rows, 48 px tab targets). Set by `pointer: coarse`, not by a preference. | The key moments (Shelf Check, Quick Add) happen on a phone, standing, often with one hand. |
| Content is text: issues, comments. | Content is covers. The panel is the plainest surface so covers carry the colour. | Covers keep their colours in both themes (canvas rule); grey chrome lets them stand out. |
| A full theme generator (custom themes). | Canvas pairs + one contrast value. No custom themes. | See below. |
| Team features: avatars, assignees, cycles. | One reader. The sidebar footer says "Private by default" instead of a workspace switcher. | Privacy is a product promise (ADR 0003); it stays visible. |
| The compose button is the main sidebar action. | "Check a book" is an outlined button on desktop and the large centre tab on the phone. | Shelf Check is a phone action; on desktop it is secondary. |

### Why not a full generator

The canvas light and dark colours do not follow one formula. As a share of ink mixed into the ground (OKLab lightness), the dark theme uses about 2 to 3 times bigger steps for fills and lines (soft fill 4.1% light against 11.6% dark; lines 7.6% against 15.3%). One formula would move the tuned canvas colours. A generator pays off only with custom themes, which Atomes does not plan. So the source stays the canvas pairs, and only the contrast step is derived.

Mix in `oklab`, not `oklch`: Chrome gives an `oklch` mix of near-greys the hue `none`, which paints as red.

## Behaviour definitions

**Breakpoint.** Desktop frame from 960 px wide. Under 960 px, phone frame. Row height follows the input type: 32 px with a fine pointer, 44 px with a coarse pointer.

**Sidebar (desktop).** 232 px, fixed. Top row: mark and "Atomes" (links to Home), Search icon button (goes to Library search). Under it: "Check a book". Then Home, Library, New & upcoming (hidden until its screen exists), To Buy, Statistics ("Statistiques", icon `chart-column`). Bottom: Settings, Sign out and the line "Private by default". On the phone, Sign out is in Settings (the You tab). The active item has a soft fill and ink text; the others have secondary text. Hover gives the soft fill. No accent colour. New & upcoming may show a count of New Volumes, in tabular figures, never a coloured dot.

**Panel (desktop).** One surface panel, 8 px from the window edges. Only the panel scrolls; the sidebar stays. A page that stops at a width (`--page-width`, `--page-wide`) is centred in the panel, so a wide screen never leaves all its free room on one side; a page of covers (Home, Library) fills the panel instead. From 1280 px wide, an overview page puts its parts side by side: Statistics shows its four tiles on one row, and from 1440 px Most read, Your Library today and Loans as three columns; the Series page puts "Where you stand" and "Missing Volumes" side by side beside the cover, and its Volumes as wide as the page. The Library List packs its columns at the left (title, author, status), and the free room is at the end of the row. On Home, the cards of a few words (the year card, the empty Library) stop at `--page-width`.

**View header.** 48 px, sticky inside the panel, hairline under it. Left: page title (15 px, 600). Detail pages (Book, Series) show a back arrow before the title, on both frames: it goes back in history when the previous page is an Atomes page, else to the Library. The installed app has no browser Back button, so the arrow is the way back. Right: actions of the page (slot `actions`). On the phone: 28 px title, not sticky; on a detail page the title is hidden and the back arrow sticks at the top as a round button on the ground (`--surface`, `--line-control`), so it is always in reach and reads over a cover; taps around it reach the page.

**View toolbar (Library only).** A second header row under the view header; it sticks on desktop, not on the phone. Desktop, first row: search, the Books / Series switch, the result count and "Clear all" when a search or a filter is on, and the Grid / List / Cover flow switch at the right. In a narrow window with a filter on, this row wraps: the mode switch goes to a second line, still at the right. Under it: "Search in", Sort, the author and Series filters, then the Reading Status, Owned and Label chips (no Label chips until the reader has a Label). Phone: Books / Series is a toolbar row, as wide as the screen. Then four round icon buttons: search, "Sort and filter" (a badge on its corner counts the filters on), the mode and Select. Icons, not words: the row must fit a 360 px screen in French, with no sideways scroll. The search icon opens the search field on a row of its own under it, as wide as the screen and focused; the icon becomes Close, which closes the field and clears the search (Escape too). The field is open from the start when the Library comes back with a search. The mode: one square button with the icon of the mode on (grid, list, gallery), a native select under it, so a tap opens the system menu of Grid, List and Cover flow. "Sort and filter" opens "Search in", Sort and the filters in a sheet. When a search or a filter is on, a row with the result count and "Clear all" comes under it. Both: with no search or filter on, no count shows, and the chips show no counts; the count stays a live region, empty, for screen readers. In List, a row shows its Reading Status only when it is not *unread* ("Not yet"). The A to Z index shows only with 20 books or more in the List, and only the letters that have books: the last row of the toolbar on desktop, a rail on the right edge on the phone. Grid, List and Cover flow are for Books: Series shows a grid of cards, with no mode switch and no index. The toolbar never changes the sidebar. Code: `src/islands/Library.tsx`, `src/pages/library.astro`.

**Tab bar (phone).** Home, Library, Check, To Buy, You. Check is a 52 px ink circle in the centre, labelled for screen readers only. The bar stays at five entries: Statistics is reached from a card on Home and a row in You (Settings). The bar sits on the surface colour with a top hairline and the bottom safe-area inset.

**Connection status.** As Linear does, the frame says when Atomes is offline; the pages do not. Desktop: a yellow box over the free space of the sidebar, right above Settings, so nothing in the sidebar moves. Phone: a yellow strip on top of the tab bar. Both read "Offline", then the number of saves waiting on this device, then a Reconnect button. Hidden while online. Offline means the browser says so, or a page could not reach the network and nothing answered since. Reconnect asks the Atomes server; if it answers, the pages read and send again at once. Caution colours (yellow) and the `wifi-off` icon, not red: Atomes still works offline, partly. Code: `src/lib/connection.ts`, `src/components/ConnectionStatus.astro`.

**Environment badge.** Outside production, the frame says which instance this is, so the reader never changes production data by mistake. A grey pill (`--soft` fill, `--secondary` text, `--font-tech` at `--type-badge`, 500, no letter spacing) reads "staging", "preview" or "local", lowercase in the strings (`en.ts`, `fr.ts`), never by CSS `text-transform`: right of the wordmark in the sidebar on desktop, centred at the top of the phone screen above the large title, and centred above the content of Flow screens, in the page so it scrolls away and covers nothing. Production shows none. Grey, not a colour role: caution yellow stays for offline, cobalt for New Volumes and links. The favicon and the installed app icon show the same mark on an orange background (see "The mark and the app icon"), and the installed app is named "Atomes staging" ("Atomes preview", "Atomes local"). The value comes from `APP_ENV` (`production` or `staging`); a Coolify pull request preview is Preview, the dev server is Local, a built image with no value is Staging. Code: `src/components/EnvironmentBadge.astro`, `src/lib/environment.ts`, `appEnvironment` in `src/lib/server/settings.ts`.

**Notices.** The result of an action (saved, waiting, refused) is a toast, over the page, so the content does not move: bottom right of the panel on desktop, above the tab bar on the phone. Errors stay until closed. A problem with what the reader typed stays next to the field. Code: `src/lib/toast.ts`.

**Page transitions.** Between screens, the frame (sidebar, tab bar) stays still and only the panel changes. Phone: a deeper page (tabs, then Series, then Book) pushes in from the right and back slides it out; between tabs, a short fade. Desktop: a small shift and fade inside the panel. A cover tapped in the Library flies to its place on the Book page, and back to its slot when the reader goes back; a slot out of sight gets no flight. None with reduced motion; browsers with no cross-document view transitions keep the plain navigation (`src/lib/page-transition.ts`, `src/styles/page-transitions.css`).

**Before the scripts run.** The page shows before its scripts and islands have run, and a tap can come in that time (a slow phone; the CI runner). A control that a script drives works without the script, or is disabled until the script is ready: a link has the right `href` (the close icon of the privacy notice gets the screen that opened it from the server, `openerHref` in `src/lib/back.ts`); a button that opens what an island draws stays disabled until the island has drawn it (To Buy "+"); a button whose handler the page script attaches stays disabled until it is attached (Settings choices, Delete my account). Check a new control with `E2E_SLOW_SCRIPTS=300` (CLAUDE.md).

**Appearance.** `color-scheme` follows the system. `data-appearance="light|dark"` on `<html>` forces one; `data-contrast="more"` raises contrast. Settings sets Appearance (System, Light, Dark) and Contrast (System, High), kept on the device; an inline script in `AppShell` applies both before first paint. High sets `data-contrast="more"`; System leaves contrast to the OS (`prefers-contrast: more`). Rule and storage: `src/lib/appearance.ts`.

**Colour roles.** Ink: text, read spines, the one look's text levels, lines and fills ("The one look"). Secondary: metadata, inactive navigation. Soft: fills, hover, active navigation. Line: hairlines, panel edge. Cobalt (`--accent`): the one main action of a screen, filled (`--accent-fill`), in light text (`--on-accent`, Q7); New and Upcoming Volumes, links, focus, a picked item in Select (a ring on the cover or the spine), the glows of Home, the Library and Statistics. The answer above the form of a typed book (an ISBN the Catalog does not know): "new" is cobalt; "already owned" stays ink. Ownership (Q8): green (`--not-owned`, with a check) when the reader does not own the book (not in the Library, Missing from a Followed Series, read but not owned), orange (`--owned`, with an alert) when they do, only where ownership is answered (the Shelf Check answer, the Book page glow, the Series page, To Buy's glow, and the "Not owned" mark `.chip-not-owned` of To Buy and the Library); text in these colours takes `--not-owned-text` or `--owned-text`, a filled button keeps dark text (`--on-ownership`). The one orange outside ownership: the ground of the Staging, Preview and Local app icons (Q12); the environment badge stays grey. On the Shelf Check result screen, neutral (ink, no glow, no rings) when the device cannot tell, and its main action is filled with the answer colour. Danger (`--danger`, a muted red): the text or icon of an action that destroys (remove a Copy, delete a Label, reset), nothing else: no fills, and not for errors, which keep ink and weight. A destructive action offers Undo in its toast; one that cannot be undone keeps the confirm dialog. Caution (yellow, `--caution` on `--caution-tint`): the offline status, nothing else. Charts (ADR 0019): ink and the medium shade only, ink (`--ink`) for "read" and `--shade-medium` for "added"; no ownership colour and no new palette, since a chart answers "how many", not "do I own it"; on Statistics, cobalt stays for the glow and the main action (Share). Covers: their own colours, with a thin edge (`--cover-edge`).

## Landing page (M12)

The public page at `/welcome` (`src/pages/welcome.astro`, frame `src/layouts/Landing.astro`; spec "Spec: M12 · Landing page"). It is not an app screen: no sidebar, tab bar, gate, environment badge or service worker. It keeps the one look:

* **Same parts.** Tokens of `theme.css`, the shared glow (`glow.css`), Schibsted Grotesk, Lucide icons through `src/components/icons.ts`, the Mark, `.button-main`, `.button`, `.card`, `.chip`. Larger type and room come from sizes the page names once (`--landing-*`); `tokens.test.ts` checks the page, its frame and `LandingShot.astro`.
* **Colour answers a question here too.** Cobalt glows behind the hero and the last call to action; green and orange only in the Shelf Check part, where ownership is answered. One filled cobalt action per area (the navbar Sign in, the hero, the end).
* **Light and dark.** Every app picture exists in both themes (`bun run landing-shots`); CSS shows the one of the theme, also when the Appearance is forced. The navbar toggle saves the same Appearance setting as Settings.
* **English and French.** Text from `t()` (`landing.*`); the app pictures and the share preview exist in both languages (`public/landing/<language>/`). The language switch (in the navbar list and the footer) names the other language in itself and sets the same choice as Settings, so sign-in and the app follow. It and the light or dark toggle stay disabled until their script is ready.
* **Phone.** The navbar links, the language switch and the light or dark toggle fold under a menu button (Escape closes it), so the bar keeps room for its call to action; with a saved Account the bar takes the short label ("Ma bibliothèque"). Feature cards go two a row; no sideways scroll at 360 px, in both languages.
* **No brand logos.** Lucide 1 has none: a link to the code uses `code-xml` and the words "Source code".
* **Room to grow.** The navbar list has Docs and Blog entries, hidden until their page exists.
