How Atomes works on AT Protocol

Your Library lives on your own server (PDS), Private by default. The Atomes instance only knows books, never who owns or reads them. Six figures, from where things run to what happens when you scan a book.

Where Atomes runs · Who can see what · What is stored where · A New Volume alert · Quick Add after buying a book · Shelf Check in a shop, with no signal

Fig. 1 · Deployment

Where Atomes runs

Three places, one rule: your Library lives on your PDS, not on the Atomes instance.

Fig. 2 · Architecture

Who can see what

Private is the default. Sharing moves a record to your public repo; nothing else is ever broadcast.

Fig. 3 · Data model

What is stored where

Your records hold only references (ISBN, ids). Missing and New Volumes are computed, never stored. Dropped Series and Release Reports are left out to keep it readable.

Fig. 4 · Sequence

A New Volume alert

The watcher only knows an Edition Line and an anonymous push subscription. Matching against your Library happens on your phone.

Fig. 5 · Sequence

Quick Add after buying a book

One scan, one tap. Series Onboarding only appears the first time a Series enters your Library.

Shelf Check in a shop, with no signal

Everything here runs on your phone against the local mirror and cached covers. The network is only needed for books you’ve never seen.
